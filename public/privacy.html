Privacy Policy

KailenFlow Suite · Effective 24 September 2026

KailenFlow Suite is a private tool used by its owner, Anthony Martinez. This policy explains what financial data it collects, why, and how it is protected and deleted.

Who this covers

The app has a single user: its owner. The only financial data it collects is the owner's own, from accounts the owner chooses to connect.

What is collected

  • Bank transactions, through Plaid, only after the owner connects an account and consents in Plaid's own window: date, amount, description, merchant, category and account identifier.
  • Payments received, from the owner's own Stripe account.

Bank usernames and passwords are never collected — they are entered only in Plaid's window and never reach this app. Account numbers and balances are not stored.

Why

To show the owner their monthly income, business and personal expenses, and profit. The data is not used for anything else, and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

How it is protected

  • Encrypted in transit (HTTPS, TLS 1.2 or better) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Available only behind the owner's login, which is protected by 2-Step Verification.
  • Access keys are stored as encrypted server settings and never sent to the browser.

Retention and deletion

Transactions are kept while an account is connected, so monthly history can be shown. Disconnecting an account revokes access at Plaid and immediately deletes every stored transaction from that account.

Third parties

Plaid (Plaid's privacy policy) connects the bank account. Netlify hosts the app and its encrypted storage. Stripe provides payment records.
